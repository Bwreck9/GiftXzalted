GOAL
When a free user clicks **Preview Questionnaire** on the Free plan card,
show a pop-up modal that looks exactly like the questionnaire form but:

- The Submit button is disabled.

     **🔒 Tokens Required**

     - **Buy Tokens** → navigate(`/pricing?highlight=buytokens`)
